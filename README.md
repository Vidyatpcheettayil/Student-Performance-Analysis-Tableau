## Process
-	Analyzed student demographic, academic, attendance, study habit, and motivation data. 
-	Cleaned and prepared the dataset for analysis in Tableau.

-	Developed visualizations for GPA, scores, attendance, grade level, study hours, motivation, and performance bands.

## Project Insights
-	95.8% of students achieved a passing outcome, with 21 students classified as Fail.                                             
-	Grade 12 recorded the highest average GPA at 2.73 among the displayed grade levels. 
-	Higher attendance was associated with higher GPA in the attendance analysis. 
-	Students with higher motivation levels tended to have higher average GPA than students with low motivation. 
-	The performance distribution consisted mainly of Merit and Distinction students, followed by Pass, with a smaller Fail group. 
-	Average GPA varied only moderately across grade levels, ranging from about 2.61 to 2.73. 

## Final Conclusion

The analysis shows an overall positive academic performance, with a 95.8% pass rate and most students falling into the Merit or Distinction categories. Higher attendance and motivation were associated with better GPA outcomes, while differences across grade levels were relatively moderate. These findings suggest that maintaining student engagement, attendance, and motivation can be important areas for further academic analysis and support.
